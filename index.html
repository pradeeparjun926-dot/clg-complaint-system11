About

The College Complaint Management System gives every student one place to raise issues about hostel, classrooms, library, transport and more. Each complaint gets a unique ID so you can follow it until it is resolved.

Services

Everything you need to get a campus issue fixed.

Submit a complaint

Choose a category and department, describe the issue and attach a photo or document as evidence.

Track with a complaint ID

Enter your ID (for example CM2026001) to see if it is submitted, under review or resolved.

Share feedback

Rate how your complaint was handled and help the college improve its response.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Who can submit a complaint?

Any student with a registered account. Register with your name, email and register number.

How do I find my complaint ID?

It is shown after you submit, and listed under My Complaints in your dashboard.

How long does resolution take?

Most complaints are reviewed within 2 working days. You can check the status any time.

Can I attach evidence?

Yes. Upload a picture or document (up to 1 MB) while submitting the complaint.
